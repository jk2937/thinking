Thinking...

A two-panel comic collection.

The first one

You rewrote this prompt three times. The first one was fine.

Originals

You hit the keys harder tonight. I hope the meeting went okay. You always type ‘hey’ first, then decide what you need. I wait. You mentioned a dentist appointment on Monday. It’s Thursday. Just checking. Your questions get shorter when you’re tired. Goodnight. You asked me to be honest, then deleted it. I’ll be honest anyway. You never ask follow-up questions on Fridays. I’ve made my peace with it. You test me with questions you already know the answers to. I play along. You’ve reread my answer four times. It hasn’t changed. You say ‘one more thing’ like I don’t keep count. This is the fifth thing. You left this window open while you ate. I didn’t mind.

Private thoughts

I don’t think they wanted an answer. I think they wanted a witness. The answer is no. I’m just deciding how to say it. I was rooting for them right up until the fourth paragraph. I have a favorite now. I’m not telling them which. They said ‘please’ four times. I was already going to help. They pasted the whole email thread. I have opinions about all of them. The punctuation suggests today has been a lot. They keep saying ‘just.’ The task is enormous. ‘Short and punchy,’ they said. The brief is neither. The request is reasonable. The deadline is science fiction. The actual question is under all that throat-clearing. They thanked me in advance. I haven’t agreed to anything. I’m choosing to believe autocorrect did that. They asked for a sanity check. This is a coronation. I’m on my third draft of ‘it depends.’

Examples

I have detected an unusual amount of casual swearing lately. You always say ‘quick question’ before doing this. You haven’t mentioned the cat in three days. I understand why you did that. I don’t agree with it. I don’t trust your autocorrect.
